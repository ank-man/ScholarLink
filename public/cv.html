💡 What makes an academic CV strong

  • Lead with research. For grad school, research experience matters more than jobs. Put it right after education.
  • Start bullets with a verb and add a number: "Analyzed 24 RNA-seq samples…", "Mentored 30 students…".
  • Say what came of it: a poster, a dataset, a thesis, a skill you can use in their lab.
  • Newest first in every section, with the same date format everywhere.
  • One to two pages is right for an undergrad. No photo, age, or GPA under 3.0 on US applications.
  • Posters count. List symposium posters and talks under Presentations, with your name in bold.
  • Ask your references first, and give them your CV and statement when you do.
