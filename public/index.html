How it works

About three minutes to sign up. The rest is two good conversations.

  1. Sign up in 3 minutesPick a department and add a few interest keywords, your goals, and when you're free.
  2. Get matched instantlyWe pair you with the best-fit mentor in that department who has room.
  3. Meet twice in 8 weeksIn person or on Zoom. Your private page shows your mentor's contact info.
  4. Log it and rate itMark meetings done and tell us how it went. That's how the program improves.

Under the hood

How a match is scored

Every mentor in your department with an open spot gets a score. Highest score wins; ties go to whoever joined first. If nobody has room, you're matched automatically the moment someone does.

Built for trust

Private by default

  • 🔒  Only @uga.edu emails can register.
  • 🔗  You get a private link to your page. Only a hash of its secret is stored.
  • 👥  Only your match sees your contact info. The site shows counts, never names.
  • 🧑‍🏫  Each department has a GSA Mentor Lead who keeps an eye on matches.
  • 🖼️  Name cards are made in your browser. Photos are never uploaded.

Questions

Who can join?

Any UGA undergraduate can sign up as a student. Any UGA graduate student (Master's, PhD, or professional) can sign up as a mentor. During the pilot, only a few departments are open; more will be added.

How much time does it take?

Two meetings over eight weeks. Most pairs meet for 30–45 minutes, in person or on Zoom. Mentors choose how many students to take, from one to three.

What if nobody in my department has room?

You'll go on the waitlist and be matched automatically as soon as a mentor joins or a spot frees up. Check your private page for updates.

What if the match isn't working?

Contact your department's GSA Mentor Lead (their email is on your private page). They can help or arrange a new match.

What are name cards for?

Bring one to orientation or your first meeting, post it to celebrate joining, or print a sheet of badges for a department mixer. Everything is drawn in your browser.
