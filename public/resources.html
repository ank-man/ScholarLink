Meeting agendas

About 45 minutes each.

Meeting 1: getting to know each other

  1. Welcome and introductions (5 min)
  2. Their story (10 min)
  3. Your story (10 min)
  4. Their goals (10 min)
  5. Expectations and confidentiality limits (5 min)
  6. Next steps, one SMART goal, a date for Meeting 2 (5 min)

Meeting 2: guidance and next steps

  1. How did the goal go? (5 min)
  2. Their main question: research, grad school, careers or balance (20 min)
  3. Feedback on a document, if they want it (10 min)
  4. Resources and next steps (5 min)
  5. Closing: how to stay in touch, then log and rate (5 min)
