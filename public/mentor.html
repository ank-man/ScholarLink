Become a ScholarLink mentor

You don't need to do more. Just share what you already know. Two conversations per student over eight weeks, at times that suit you.

Two meetings per student. You choose how many students (1–3).

A participation certificate, leadership recognition, and a feature highlighting your contribution.

Volunteer to help undergrads with grad school applications: SOP reads, CV tips, and more.
