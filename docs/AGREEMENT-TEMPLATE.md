# Memorandum of Understanding (template)

**Between:** Ankush Sharma ("Developer") and the Graduate Student Association of the University of Georgia ("GSA"),
with the University of Georgia's EITS and ELS as reviewing offices.
**Subject:** Operation of ScholarLink, the GSA Bulldog Bridge mentoring platform, and its mentor training.
**Term:** five years from the date signed, renewable by agreement.

> Template for discussion. Have it reviewed by GSA's advisor, UGA Legal Affairs and Innovation Gateway before signing.

1. **License to GSA.** The Developer grants GSA a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, host,
   modify and run ScholarLink for UGA students, staff and alumni, including the mentor training materials (handbook, slides and videos).
2. **Ownership.** The Developer keeps the copyright in the software and the ScholarLink name and logo. GSA keeps its
   own content and the UGA hosting accounts. Participants own their data.
3. **Commercial rights.** The Developer may offer ScholarLink, or a product based on it, to others, including
   commercially. Doing so does not reduce GSA's rights under this agreement.
4. **Founding partner.** If a paid or hosted edition is offered, GSA and UGA receive it at no cost or at a discount
   for at least five years.
5. **Data.** No UGA user data is transferred to any other service or company without each user's consent. GSA may
   export all UGA data at any time.
6. **Costs.** Hosting runs on free plans. GSA or ELS funds any optional costs (printing, a custom domain) as budgeted. No University
   funds pay for software development, and no fees are paid to the Developer.
7. **University marks.** UGA names and marks are used only in the UGA deployment, as UGA's brand guidelines allow.
8. **Support and transition.** The Developer maintains the software in good faith while a UGA student. On leaving,
   the Developer gives at least 60 days' notice, hands over hosting documentation and helps a successor for 90 days.
9. **Ending.** Either party may end this agreement with 90 days' written notice. Sections 1, 2, 5 and 8 survive.
10. **No warranty.** The software is provided as is, under the MIT license.
