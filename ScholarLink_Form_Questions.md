# ScholarLink Registration Forms

Two forms for the Bulldog Bridge pilot: one for undergraduate mentees, one for graduate mentors.
Build them in Google Forms or Microsoft Forms using the questions below **in this order**. The exported
responses can then be pasted into `ScholarLink_Pilot.xlsx` starting at column B (Timestamp), with every column lined up.

**Copy answer choices exactly**, including spelling and capitalization. The workbook scores matches by
looking for these exact phrases. If you add or rename a choice, update the same list on the workbook's **Settings** sheet.

Turn on "Collect email addresses" so email becomes the column right after Timestamp.

## Form 1: ScholarLink Mentee Registration (Undergraduates)

**Form description:**
> ScholarLink, part of Bulldog Bridge (an SGA × GSA initiative), pairs undergraduates with graduate student
> mentors in their field of interest. The commitment is light: two meetings over eight weeks. A GSA Mentor Lead in your
> chosen department will review matches and introduce you to your mentor by email.

| # | Question | Type | Required | Choices |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | Email | Collected automatically | Yes | — |
| 1 | Full name | Short answer | Yes | — |
| 2 | Year | Multiple choice | Yes | Freshman · Sophomore · Junior · Senior |
| 3 | Major | Short answer | Yes | — |
| 4 | Which department would you like a mentor from? | Dropdown | Yes | *Your pilot departments, exactly as listed on Settings* (e.g., Computer Science · Psychology · Genetics · Marketing) |
| 5 | Interest keyword 1 | Short answer | Yes | Help text: "One topic, 1–3 words (e.g., machine learning, ecology, public health)" |
| 6 | Interest keyword 2 | Short answer | No | Same help text |
| 7 | Interest keyword 3 | Short answer | No | Same help text |
| 8 | What are your career goals? (select all that apply) | Checkboxes | Yes | Graduate school (PhD) · Graduate school (Master's) · Medical / Professional school · Industry R&D · Industry (non-research) · Government / Public sector · Nonprofit · Entrepreneurship · Undecided |
| 9 | When are you usually available? (select all that apply) | Checkboxes | Yes | Weekday mornings · Weekday afternoons · Weekday evenings · Weekends |
| 10 | Preferred meeting format | Multiple choice | Yes | In person · Virtual · Either |
| 11 | What do you hope to get from a mentor? | Paragraph | No | — |
| 12 | I can commit to two meetings with my mentor over eight weeks. | Multiple choice | Yes | Yes |

**Tip for keywords:** short, common terms match best. "machine learning" will find a mentor whose research says
"machine learning for medical imaging", but "ML-based diagnostics" won't.

## Pilot workflow (for GSA Mentor Leads)

1. **Weekly:** export new responses and paste them into the Mentees and Mentors sheets below the last filled row.
2. **Match:** on **Match Finder**, choose each mentee whose status is *Waiting*, review the top 3, and pick one.
3. **Log it:** on **Matches**, enter the mentee ID and mentor ID, set Status to *Proposed*, then *Introduced* once the intro email goes out.
4. **Follow up:** record meeting dates as they happen. Status → *Active* after meeting 1 and *Completed* after meeting 2.
5. **Feedback:** after meeting 2, ask both people for a 1–5 rating and enter it.
6. **Report:** share the **Dashboard** with the central GSA team and SGA at each check-in.

### Sample introduction email

> **Subject:** ScholarLink intro: [Mentee] ↔ [Mentor]
>
> Hi [Mentee] and [Mentor],
>
> I'm [Lead], the GSA Mentor Lead for [Department]. You've been matched through ScholarLink!
> [Mentee] is a [year] in [major] interested in [keywords]. [Mentor] is a [program] student working on [research area].
>
> The plan is two conversations over the next eight weeks, in person or virtual, whichever works best for you.
> [Mentee], could you reach out to [Mentor] with a few times that work for you?
>
> Thanks for being part of Bulldog Bridge!
> [Lead]
